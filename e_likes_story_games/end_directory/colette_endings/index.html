COLETTE ENDING 1

This is the base Colette ending, (Theoretically), if you do not change any of Colette's preferred choices, you will end up here.
  • DAY 1:
    • At ~3:00PM, when you are approached by a shady guy on the street, say NO!

  • DAY 2:
    • When Uncle asks for lunch over the phone (~12:00PM), say yes.
    • At ~3:00PM, when you spot a long haired boy, don't talk to him.
    • Make sure you have made a deal with the Lady in Green by the end of this day, and that you have snuck out of the Casino. (Sort of end of this day, it's like ~4:00AM the NEXT DAY, but yknow, before Colette sleeps.)
      • Again, by default this will happen, so don't worry too much lol.

  • DAY 3:
    • AT ~2:00PM - 2:30PM!!
    • Colette will go to the Library with Wren.
      • THIS can only happen if WREN does NOT decide to go back to Rowan's place to eat lunch.
      • Wren by default will not be with Rowan, but if you are simultaneously also trying for Wren's Rowan endings in the same playthrough, make sure Wren does NOT go back to Rowan.
    • She leaves the library at 7:00PM to go back to the Casino.
    • While there, make sure you Don't Point The Gun.
    • This will lead to ENDING 1

  • DAY 2:

COLETTE ENDING 4

This ending relies on certain choices on JIN'S SIDE.
  • DAY 1:
    • At ~3:00PM, when you are approached by a shady guy on the street, say NO!
    • ON JIN'S SIDE - overall, you must make sure that Jin doesn't overtly favor Gou or Mao. By default this should be true, but it can change depending on your actions with certain choices and during his IDLE DRESS REHEARSAL SEQUENCES...

  • DAY 2:
    • When Uncle asks for lunch over the phone (~12:00PM), say yes.
    • At ~3:00PM, when you spot a long haired boy, MAKE SURE YOU TALK TO HIM.
    • Make sure you have made a deal with the Lady in Green by the end of this day, and that you have snuck out of the Casino. (Sort of end of this day, it's like ~4:00AM the NEXT DAY, but yknow, before Colette sleeps.)
      • Again, by default this will happen, so don't worry too much lol.
    • ON JIN'S SIDE, YOU NEED TO MAKE SURE HE DOES NOT OVERDOSE.
      • By default he will, ensure he doesn't by making him NOT buy any more pills or NOT taking any after he buys them.
      • This is around 6:30PM or 7:30PM.

  • DAY 3:
    • AT ~2:00PM - 2:30PM!!
    • Colette will go to the Library with Wren.
      • THIS can only happen if WREN does NOT decide to go back to Rowan's place to eat lunch.
      • Wren by default will not be with Rowan, but if you are simultaneously also trying for Wren's Rowan endings in the same playthrough, make sure Wren does NOT go back to Rowan.
    • At 7:00PM, Colette will NOT receive any urgent text, instead they will leisurely come back from the Library and go to the Shady Den.
    • There will be no one there, so she decides to make a PHONE CALL.
    • This should lead to ENDING 4

COLETTE ENDING 6

This ending RELIES on JIN's SIDE also making certain choices.
  • DAY 1:
    • At ~3:00PM, when you are approached by a shady guy on the street, say NO!
    • ON JIN'S SIDE - overall, you must make sure that Jin doesn't overtly favor Gou or Mao. By default this should be true, but it can change depending on your actions with certain choices and during his IDLE DRESS REHEARSAL SEQUENCES...

  • DAY 2:
    • When Uncle asks for lunch over the phone (~12:00PM), say NO!!
    • If Jin doesn't overtly favor Gou or Mao, then they will show up to eat with Colette. Otherwise the route will turn back to the standard path (Endings 1 - 5 and 8)
    • During dinner with the Lady in Green (~5:50PM) you will have a choice this time if you want to accept her offer, SAY NO!!!
      • If you say yes, it will turn back to the standard path (Endings 1-5 and 8)
    • Colette will miss half of her shift to go see Gou and Mao.
    • ON JIN'S SIDE, YOU NEED TO MAKE SURE HE DOES NOT OVERDOSE.
      • By default he will, ensure he doesn't by making him NOT buy any more pills or NOT taking any after he buys them.
      • This is around 6:30PM or 7:30PM.
    • After the performance (11:00PM) make sure Colette gets Gou and Mao's number.
    • And then she will go to sleep at the CASINO, and not the Shady Den.

  • DAY 3:
    • This will lead you to ENDING 6
