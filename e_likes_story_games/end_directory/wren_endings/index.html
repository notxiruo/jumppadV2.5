WREN ENDING 1

This is the base Wren ending, if you do not change any of Wren's preferred choices, you will end up here.
  • DAY 1:
    • Ignore the Father and the Daughter, do not speak up.
      • Or you can just choose to not go with them on the morning of DAY 2.

  • DAY 2:
    • Get away from the Prodigy (aka. NIGHTMARE or ROWAN) by just... choosing to leave or ending the conversation.
      • Wren will do this even if you say yes only once/twice/thrice. BASICALLY, it's harder to get them to go with Rowan than it is to get them to leave Rowan
      • IF you have agreed to go with Rowan and then decide to DITCH him after going to grab your stuff on your own, you will MISS this ending! (That is a different ending.)
    • Whether you get into a fight with Jin or not does not change any ending.
    • Make sure when you go to bed at the end of DAY 2, that you are sleeping BY YOURSELF.

  • DAY 3:
    • At the end of DAY 3, tell the Prodigy (Rowan) that it's "None of his business."
    • You will reach ENDING 1

WREN ENDING 3

This is one of the endings where you end up going with Rowan.
  • DAY 1:
    • Ignore the Father and the Daughter, do not speak up.
      • Or you can just choose to not go with them on the morning of DAY 2.

  • DAY 2:
    • When the Prodigy (aka. NIGHTMARE or ROWAN) appears, (this is ~12:00PM after Wren's busking sequence) keep forcing Wren to stay and chat with him.
      • You have to do this four (4) times I think? Wren will try to get away at every chance they've got. So just saying "yes" once and then jumping away will not set Wren down the right path, they WILL leave on their own if you don't lock them in.
      • They are locked in to spend time with Rowan IF you agree to let Rowan come along with picking up Wren's stuff, OR you choose to NOT ditch Rowan if Wren goes to pick up their stuff on their own.
    • Side note: Going with Rowan means you will miss the encounter on DAY 2 with Jin.
    • Make sure when you go to bed at the end of DAY 2, that you are sleeping IN ROWAN'S APARTMENT.

  • DAY 3:
    • When you are done busking on DAY 3 (this is ~2:00PM) choose to NOT go back to Rowan's place. Wren will end up spending time at the library instead.
    • Once Wren has left the library (this is ~7:00PM) they will go back to Rowan's Apartment.
    • This will lead to ENDING 3

WREN ENDING 7

This is one of the endings where you end up going with the Father and Daughter.
  • DAY 1:
    • Speak up at the end of the day (around 8:00PM) when the Father and Daughter are being confronted by the Tunnel Folk.

  • DAY 2:
    • The Father will approach you in the morning (around 7:00AM) and ask if you want to stay with them for a while, and you should SAY YES here.
      • By default Wren will say NO, so if you do not jump to Wren and say yes here, they will head back on the default path. (Towards endings 1 - 6 and 9)
      • Yea, so if you reply NO there, you will abort the Father and Daughter route and go back to the other ending routes.
    • Side note: Going with Liam and Millie means you will miss the encounter on DAY 2 with Rowan.
    • Side note: Going with Liam and Millie means you will miss the encounter on DAY 2 with Jin.
    • Endings 7 and 8 are determined by how many 'points' you score with Millie. You can earn points with Millie by:
      • Choosing to sit in the back with Millie.
      • Eating off the kids menu.
      • You will LOSE TWO POINTS if you choose to order the most expensive item off the menu.
    • Make sure when you go to bed at the end of DAY 2, that you are sleeping IN THE CAR.

  • DAY 3:
    • Endings 7 and 8 are determined by how many 'points' you score with Millie. You can earn points with Millie by:
      • Watching Millie when Liam has to go back to his part-time job.
    • There are 3 total points you can earn with Millie.
    • If you have earned LESS THAN 2 POINTS, then you will reach ENDING 7
