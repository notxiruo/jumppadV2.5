JIN AND COLETTE ENDING 1

These two endings are Super Cool Secret Endings! Wow! The difference between Ending 1 and Ending 2 is based on whether or not Jin and Colette are BOTH aware of YOUR existence. ENDING 1 is based on them NOT knowing.
  • COLETTE SIDE DAY 1:
    • At ~3:00PM, when you are approached by a shady guy on the street, say NO!

  • JIN SIDE DAY 1:
    • Do whatever you want, babygirl.
    • Note: If you are NOT on your first playthrough, do NOT tell Jin your name if it prompts you.
    • Recommended that you just stay jumped in Colette's side on DAY 1 while working to get this ending.

  • WREN SIDE DAY 2:
    • When the Prodigy (aka. NIGHTMARE or ROWAN) appears, (this is ~12:00PM after Wren's busking sequence) keep forcing Wren to stay and chat with him.
      • You have to do this four (4) times I think? Wren will try to get away at every chance they've got. So just saying "yes" once and then jumping away will not set Wren down the right path, they WILL leave on their own if you don't lock them in.
      • They are locked in to spend time with Rowan IF you agree to let Rowan come along with picking up Wren's stuff, OR you choose to NOT ditch Rowan if Wren goes to pick up their stuff on their own.
    • Basically, you need to lock Wren onto Rowan's route for this ending to work.
    • Make sure when you go to bed at the end of DAY 2, that you are sleeping IN ROWAN'S APARTMENT.

  • COLETTE SIDE DAY 2:
    • When Uncle asks for lunch over the phone (~12:00PM), say yes.
      • (Default Colette will say yes, so you don't need to jump here and do that)
    • At ~3:00PM, when you spot a long haired boy, TALK TO HIM!!!
    • Make sure you have made a deal with the Lady in Green by the end of this day, and that you have snuck out of the Casino. (Sort of end of this day, it's like ~4:00AM the NEXT DAY, but yknow, before Colette sleeps.)
      • Again, by default this will happen, so don't worry too much lol.

  • JIN SIDE DAY 2:
    • AT 10:00AM, Eden will ask you some questions in the form of a personality test.
      • Do not flip any of the levers, answer them as if you are Jin.
    • MAKE SURE JIN DOES NOT OVERDOSE by the end of today. There are multiple ways of doing this depending on where you end up, so don't stress too much if you Miss the Timeslot or something.
      • Stop Jin from going to the Lady (~6:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from buying any pills from the Lady (~7:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from taking any pills if he bought some (~9:00PM)
    • MAKE SURE JIN TELLS THE TRUTH TO EDEN about how many bottles he bought.

  • DAY 2 COMPREHENSIVE:
    • Because reading this can be tricky, here's A METHOD you can use for DAY 2:
    • Stick with Wren, make sure that they end up going with Rowan to Rowan's apartment. You can ensure this all the way to when Wren and Rowan get on the BUS together at 3:00PM.
    • THEN right at 3:00PM, jump over to Colette's side and you should be right at the moment where she would see the Long Haired Boy. Then make sure she talks to him.
    • After the long haired boy and her part ways, jump over to Jin's side and just make sure he doesn't overdose before he goes to sleep.

  • WREN SIDE DAY 3:
    • The only thing you need to make sure on Wren's side for this is that they are going to get lunch with ROWAN after busking.
    • This is a choice that you make while jumped into their brain at 2:20PM.

  • COLETTE SIDE DAY 3:
    • Right after you make the choice to go to lunch with Rowan, jump over to Colette's side and she should decide to call EDEN.
      • She would usually end up going to the library with Wren, but because Wren is NOT available, she will check her other choices-- and in this case, the choice to call Eden would be available due to her having met him previously.
      • And Eden will be available to talk because Jin did not overdose.
    • This should lead you to the combined ENDING 1

  • JIN SIDE DAY 3:
    • AT 2:30PM, Eden will get a call from Colette.
    • This should lead to the combined ENDING 1
