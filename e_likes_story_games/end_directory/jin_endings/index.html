JIN ENDING 1

This is the base Jin ending, If you do not change any of Jin's preferred choices, you will end up here.
  • DAY 1:
    • Do whatever you want, babygirl.
    • Note: If you are NOT on your first playthrough, do NOT tell Jin your name if it prompts you.

  • DAY 2:
    • AT 10:00AM, Eden will ask you some questions in the form of a personality test.
      • Do not flip any of the levers, answer them as if you are Jin.
    • If Jin gets into a fight with Wren or not, it does not matter, it just changes when Jin is able to get back to rehearsal. Make sure he goes to the Lady to restock on his pills.
    • Make sure Jin Overdoses by the end of today. (This will happen normally without your input.)

  • DAY 3:
    • At the hospital, Eden will ask you some personality test questions again. (The same ones.) Make sure you once again answer as if you are Jin and DO NOT flip any of the levers.
    • Note: If you are NOT on your first playthrough, once again, do NOT tell Jin your name if it prompts you.
    • This should lead you to ENDING 1

JIN ENDING 3

This is part of the Jin Endings that rely on him NOT overdosing.
  • DAY 1:
    • Do whatever you want, babygirl.
    • Note: If you are NOT on your first playthrough, do NOT tell Jin your name if it prompts you.
    • OVERALL -- you must make sure that Jin doesn't overtly favor Gou or Mao. By default this should be true, but it can change depending on your actions with certain choices and during his IDLE DRESS REHEARSAL SEQUENCES...

  • DAY 2:
    • AT 10:00AM, Eden will ask you some questions in the form of a personality test.
      • Do not flip any of the levers, answer them as if you are Jin.
    • If Jin gets into a fight with Wren or not, it does not matter, it just changes when Jin is able to get back to rehearsal.
    • MAKE SURE JIN DOES NOT OVERDOSE by the end of today. There are multiple ways of doing this depending on where you end up, so don't stress too much if you Miss the Timeslot or something.
      • Stop Jin from going to the Lady (~6:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from buying any pills from the Lady (~7:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from taking any pills if he bought some (~9:00PM)
    • MAKE SURE JIN TELLS THE TRUTH TO EDEN about how many bottles he bought.

  • DAY 3:
    • AT 2:30PM, Jin and Eden will wrap up dress rehearsal and go eat something together.
      • This will NOT happen IF: Colette and Eden had met before AND Wren is out with lunch with Rowan.
      • Both of these things do not happen by default.
    • This should lead to ENDING 3

JIN ENDING 6

This is part of the Jin Endings that rely on him NOT overdosing... on the second day. And also one that features Gou and Mao more.
  • DAY 1:
    • Do whatever you want, babygirl.
      • Yes, you can even tell Jin your name if it prompts you and you want to.
    • OVERALL, YOU MUST MAKE SURE YOU FAVOR MAO MORE THAN GOU
      • You can do this by deliberately not increasing any Gou and Jin bond points (like when Gou tries to silently ask for help during the Meet and Greet DAY 1)
      • Or more easily, you can favor Mao more by always choosing the option to "Tell the boy to shut it" during Jin's dress rehearsal sequences.
      • The text before choosing to "Watch + 10 Minutes" during dress rehearsal sequences should indicate who is currently more favored.

  • DAY 2:
    • AT ~2:00PM, if Jin favors GOU or MAO more than the other (MAO in this case for this ending) they will be FIGHTING.
      • Make sure you do NOT resolve this fight!
      • If you resolve it, their point totals will become balanced again.
    • If Jin gets into a fight with Wren or not, it does not matter, it just changes when Jin is able to get back to rehearsal.
    • MAKE SURE JIN DOES NOT OVERDOSE by the end of today. There are multiple ways of doing this depending on where you end up, so don't stress too much if you Miss the Timeslot or something.
      • Stop Jin from going to the Lady (~6:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from buying any pills from the Lady (~7:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from taking any pills if he bought some (~9:00PM)
    • MAKE SURE JIN TELLS THE TRUTH TO EDEN about how many bottles he bought.

  • DAY 3:
    • AT 2:30PM, Jin and Eden will wrap up dress rehearsal and go eat something together.
      • This will NOT happen IF: Colette and Eden had met before AND Wren is out with lunch with Rowan.
      • Both of these things do not happen by default.
    • AT ~8:20PM, Mao will come knocking on Jin and Eden's RV door. Here you should choose to let Mao PERFORM BY HERSELF.
    • This should lead to ENDING 6

JIN ENDING 7

This is part of the Jin Endings that rely on him NOT overdosing... on the second day. And also one that features Gou and Mao more.
  • DAY 1:
    • Do whatever you want, babygirl.
      • Yes, you can even tell Jin your name if it prompts you and you want to.
    • OVERALL, YOU MUST MAKE SURE YOU FAVOR EITHER MAO OR GOU OVER THE OTHER
      • You can do this by deliberately by choosing certain responses (like when Gou tries to silently ask for help during the Meet and Greet DAY 1)
      • Or more easily, you can favor either more by repeatedly selecting the right choice during Jin's dress rehearsal sequences.
        • "Tell the boy to shut it" adds points to Mao
        • "Reassure Gou" adds points to Gou.
      • The text before choosing to "Watch + 10 Minutes" during dress rehearsal sequences should indicate who is currently more favored.

  • DAY 2:
    • AT ~2:00PM, if Jin favors GOU or MAO more than the other they will be FIGHTING.
      • Make sure you do NOT resolve this fight!
      • If you resolve it, their point totals will become balanced again.
    • If Jin gets into a fight with Wren or not, it does not matter, it just changes when Jin is able to get back to rehearsal.
    • MAKE SURE JIN DOES NOT OVERDOSE by the end of today. There are multiple ways of doing this depending on where you end up, so don't stress too much if you Miss the Timeslot or something.
      • Stop Jin from going to the Lady (~6:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from buying any pills from the Lady (~7:30PM)
      • Stop Jin from taking any pills if he bought some (~9:00PM)
    • MAKE SURE JIN TELLS THE TRUTH TO EDEN about how many bottles he bought.

  • DAY 3:
    • AT 2:30PM, Jin and Eden will wrap up dress rehearsal and go eat something together.
      • This will NOT happen IF: Colette and Eden had met before AND Wren is out with lunch with Rowan.
      • Both of these things do not happen by default.
    • AT ~8:20PM, depending on who you favor more, they will come knocking on Jin and Eden's RV Door.
      • If it is MAO who comes knocking, make sure you DON'T let her perform by herself and instead go look for Gou WITH her.
    • This should lead to ENDING 7

  • Side Note:
    • If you would like to see all of this Gou and Mao stuff in the most efficient way, I recommend you get ENDING 6 first! And then reload from your last choice in ENDING 6 to get ENDING 7 by making the opposite choice!
    • Then go back and favor GOU more to get ENDING 7 again, but with GOU knocking at your door instead of Mao. (It's a WHOLE nother fully voiced scene so I do recommend you get this ending twice for the sake of that!)
